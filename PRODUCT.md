# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS with embedded Plotly figures, generated by Python (`src/framed/report.py`); no build step. The page is copied into the owner's GitHub Pages portfolio (alfredchettiar.github.io), so it must inherit that site's tokens and fonts.

## Users
Primary: hiring managers and recruiters screening for hybrid CAE + ML roles, who need the result in under a minute, often on a phone. Secondary: CAE / ML engineers who open the detail to judge whether the method and the numbers hold up.

## Product Purpose
A benchmark report for the structural-surrogate-optimizer project: it validates a published, peer-reviewed study (FRAMED, Regenwetter et al., Computer-Aided Design 2023) from its released data, reproduces the paper's surrogate benchmark, and shows where this project's physics-guided surrogates improve on it. Success: a reader can see in 60 seconds what was reproduced, what improved and by how much, and can trace every number to its source.

## Positioning
Not a self-graded demo on synthetic data: every claim is checked against a reputed external source (MIT DeCoDE Lab dataset and paper), with paper value, reproduced value and deviation side by side, and honest discrepancies kept visible.

## Operating Context
Reached from the portfolio's project card (P-01) or the GitHub README. Read on desktop by engineers and on phones by recruiters. Figures are interactive Plotly.

## Product Principles
1. Every number traces to a JSON artefact or to the paper reference file.
2. Paper / ours / deviation, always side by side.
3. Discrepancies and limitations stay next to the results they qualify.
4. Headline first, method on demand.
